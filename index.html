Make 4:3 video fit any screen.

POV turns 4:3 footage into 16:9 landscape or 9:16 portrait. Instead of cropping away half the picture or stretching everything evenly, it keeps the middle of the shot natural and reshapes the edges to fit. Everything runs on your phone.

Natural
The middle of the shot is usually where action is, so that’s where POV changes the least. The distortion happens toward the edges, where it’s hardest to notice.
Universal
Make a landscape version for YouTube and the TV, and a portrait version for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, from the same clip.
Private
All processing happens on your device. Your videos are never uploaded, so they stay as private as the rest of your photo library.
