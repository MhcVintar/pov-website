Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

In short: POV reframes your videos entirely on your device. Your videos never leave your phone, and we don’t collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

This policy explains how the POV app (“POV”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles your information. POV is made by Miha Vintar, an independent developer. Where something works differently on a particular platform, it’s noted in that section.

Your videos

To reframe a video, you pick one from your photo library. POV uses it only to process the video, show its file name on screen and make a thumbnail of it. POV doesn’t look at, change or delete anything else in your library.

All processing happens on your device. Your videos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

The reframed video is saved to a temporary file on your device so you can preview it. It is deleted when you’re done with it, and the system may also clear it at any time. The video goes somewhere else only if you choose to save or share it using the Share button, in which case it is handled by the app or service you pick.

The reframed video keeps the original recording date, so it appears in the right place in your library. Other metadata from the original, such as location, is not copied over.

On iPhone: POV asks for access to your photo library so it can open the video you pick. You can change or revoke this access at any time in the Settings app, or from Permissions in the app’s menu.

What’s stored on your device

POV stores only two small pieces of information, both on your device:

  • How many free reframes you’ve used. This is a single number.
  • Whether you’ve bought Unlimited reframes. This is saved so the app can show the right screen straight away. Your app store always has the final say.

Neither is linked to your identity or sent to us. Depending on your device settings, this information may be included in your device backups.

On iPhone: the free reframe count is kept in your device’s Keychain, so it isn’t reset if you delete and reinstall the app. Backups, including iCloud backups, are managed by Apple.

Purchases

Unlimited reframes is an in-app purchase handled entirely by the app store you got POV from. We don’t receive your name, payment details or account information. The app checks with the store only to find out whether the purchase is active, including when you use Restore Purchases.

On iPhone: purchases go through Apple’s App Store and are covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics

If you’ve chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, your platform provider may give us crash reports and anonymous usage statistics, such as how many people use the app. This information doesn’t identify you, and you can turn it off at any time.

On iPhone: this is controlled under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message, such as screenshots. We use this only to reply to you and to fix problems, and we don’t share it with anyone. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

This website

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services receive standard technical information such as your IP address when you visit. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

POV doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If POV changes in a way that affects your privacy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
